# Data Policy & Governance (`DATA POLICY.md`)

**Project:** AI-Assisted Climate Adaptation Investment Prioritization  
**Target Geography:** Pune, Maharashtra, India  
**Scope:** Hackathon MVP (PCCOE International Grand Challenge 2026)  
**Status:** Authoritative Data Governance Policy  

---

## 1. Core Principles & Governance

This document establishes the mandatory data policy, provenance classification, licensing terms, and ethical constraints for all data used, ingested, transformed, and generated by the Climate Adaptation Investment Prioritization platform.

### Strict Non-Negotiable Grounding Rules
1. **MVP Boundary:** The MVP is strictly focused on six illustrative Pune areas (`Z01`–`Z06`) and twelve adaptation intervention packages (`P01`–`P12`, 2 mutually exclusive options per area).
2. **Hazard Scope:** The primary hazards modeled are **Monsoon Flooding** and **Extreme Urban Heat**, compounded by exposure, socioeconomic vulnerability, and critical infrastructure sensitivity.
3. **Objective Presets:** The system evaluates portfolios against four deterministic policy presets (*Flood Priority*, *Heat Priority*, *Balanced Resilience*, *Social & Climate Equity*).
4. **Budget Constraint:** Investment selection must strictly respect the available user-specified capital budget in Indian Rupees Crore (₹ Cr).
5. **Transparency & Portability:** All optimization outputs must provide human-interpretable trade-off explanations and one-click CSV export.
6. **Explicit Provenance Labeling:** Simulated data must always be explicitly labeled as `simulated` in data tables, UI cards, and export files.
7. **No False Authenticity:** Never present simulated values as verified Pune measurements or official municipal ground truth.
8. **No Unvalidated Risk Claims:** Recommendations must not claim validated real-world lives saved, exact casualty prevention, or verified physical risk reduction percentages.
9. **Advisory Decision Support:** Any optimization result must be treated as an advisory decision-support aid to guide municipal discourse, never as an automated executive mandate replacing on-ground geotechnical, engineering, and socio-economic validation.

## 2. Three-Tier Data Provenance Taxonomy

Every data point in the repository must be cataloged under one of three strict provenance tiers:

| Tier | Status Tag | Definition & Criteria | Permissible Uses & Disclaimers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | `verified` | Directly sourced from authoritative, verifiable public records (e.g., Census of India, Survey of India, officially published PMC gazettes, verified GPS coordinates). | Can be presented as factual baseline municipal context. Source citation mandatory. |
| **Tier 2** | `derived` | Computed through deterministic mathematical, GIS, or statistical operations using Tier 1 inputs (e.g., population density = verified population / GIS ward area). | Methodology and base formulas must be documented and reproducible. |
| **Tier 3** | `simulated` | Realistic, literature-informed synthetic values generated for prototype modeling, testing, and algorithmic demonstration (e.g., relative vulnerability indices, intervention package costs, and benefit points). | Must carry a prominent visual indicator (`simulated` badge). Cannot be represented as official empirical measurements. |

## 3. Data Directory Organization & Lifecycle

Data assets are partitioned into three lifecycle stages under `data/`:

```text
data/
├── raw/            # Original, immutable datasets as acquired or ingested
├── processed/      # Cleaned, standardized, normalized, and validated datasets
├── reference/      # Canonical lookup tables (zone definitions, intervention catalogs)
└── README.md       # Directory manifest, schema contracts, and ingestion logs
```

### Stage Rules
- **`data/raw/`**:
  - Read-only once placed.
  - No manual edits or ad-hoc cleaning in-place.
  - Accompanied by provenance metadata (origin URL, date retrieved, acquisition method).
- **`data/processed/`**:
  - Produced via reproducible transformation scripts from `data/raw/` or validated generation routines.
  - Normalized to standard column names, coordinate reference systems (WGS84 / EPSG:4326), and integer bounds (0–100).
- **`data/reference/`**:
  - Authoritative reference fixtures (`zones.csv`, `projects.csv`).
  - Strict schema compliance enforced by runtime validators.

## 4. Current Datasets & Provenance Manifest

### 4.1 Zone Dataset (`data/reference/zones.csv` & `data/zones.csv`)
- **Row Count:** Exactly 6 illustrative planning zones.
- **Centroids:** Approximate centroid coordinates of recognizable Pune localities (Sangamwadi, Shivajinagar, Hadapsar, Katraj, Yerawada, Hinjawadi).
- **Provenance Status:** `simulated` (Centroids are real Pune coordinates; vulnerability, exposure, and hazard indices are simulated illustrative values grounded in Pune environmental literature).
- **Reference Literature:** PMC Environment Status Reports (ESR), academic urban heat island publications for Pune, and Mula-Mutha flood basin studies.

### 4.2 Project Packages Dataset (`data/reference/projects.csv` & `data/projects.csv`)
- **Row Count:** Exactly 12 adaptation packages (2 per zone, mutually exclusive).
- **Costs:** Sized in ₹ Crore (₹1.4 Cr to ₹4.8 Cr), modeling realistic municipal pilot capital tranches.
- **Provenance Status:** `simulated` (Estimates of capital costs, relative flood benefit points, heat benefit points, equity points, and critical infrastructure protection).
- **Typologies:** Nature-Based Solutions (NBS), passive cooling, grey infrastructure, and hybrid bioswale/drainage systems.

## 5. Prohibition on Hallucinated or Invented Sources

1. **No Fictitious Citations:** Never generate synthetic citations, imaginary journal articles, or fabricated municipal department memos.
2. **Missing Data Handling:** If real-world empirical data for a ward or intervention does not exist, use a documented simulated parameter tagged `simulated`, with an explicit explanation in `source_note`.
3. **Data Integrity Checks:** All CSV files are validated by `src/data_loader.py` (or `src/data_processing/`) for valid `data_status` values (`verified`, `derived`, `simulated`). Any unauthorized status triggers an immediate `DataValidationError`.
